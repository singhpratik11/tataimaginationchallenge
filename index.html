Why it works

Zero effort

After sign-up, the rider does nothing.

Trusted

Never holds money, never pushes a loan, data never goes to delivery platforms.

Proven nudge

~16%

In bank trials, savings reminders that named a real goal raised savings by about 16% (Karlan et al.).
